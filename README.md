# 💰 Daily Expense Tracker

### Track Your Spending. Manage Your Money. Build Better Habits.

Daily Expense Tracker is a personal finance application designed to help users record daily expenses, organize spending, and understand their financial habits. The project focuses on simplifying expense management through a straightforward and user-friendly digital experience.

## 🎯 Project Objective

Managing daily expenses is essential for maintaining good financial habits. However, tracking small purchases and understanding spending patterns can become difficult without a consistent system.

Daily Expense Tracker aims to make expense recording easier, improve awareness of spending habits, and help users manage their everyday finances more effectively.

## ✨ Key Features

- **➕ Expense Management** - Record daily expenses and keep track of spending.
- **🏷️ Expense Categorization** - Organize expenses into categories for easier monitoring.
- **📅 Date-Based Tracking** - Associate expenses with dates to maintain a spending history.
- **📊 Expense Summary** - Review recorded expenses and understand total spending.
- **📋 Transaction History** - Keep expense records organized for future reference.
- **📱 User-Friendly Interface** - Provide a simple experience for everyday expense tracking.
